Our Story

Small kitchen,
big flavours

Tucked into Shadman Town on Shahrah Noor Jahan, #EAT started with one goal: make food so good people come back without being told to. Our Pizza Fries went viral before we had a proper sign. Our Zingo Burgers became a neighbourhood obsession. And our Chowmein? Regulars say it's the best in the city — and they keep proving it by ordering again.
